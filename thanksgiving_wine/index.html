Thanksgiving Day Chilling & Decanting Timeline
  1. 11:00 AM (Morning Chill): Refrigerate 2022 Marine Layer Brut Rosé, NV Mumm Napa Brut Prestige, 2023 Jericho Canyon Sauvignon Blanc, 2023 Matthiasson Chardonnay Linda Vista, 2024 Marine Layer Chardonnay Aries, 2023 Dr. Loosen Riesling, 2023 Novelty Hill Late Harvest Sémillon (375 mL), and 2025 Quails' Gate Late Harvest Riesling (375 mL).
  2. 2:00 PM (Red Cellar Prep): Stand 2023 Matthiasson Pinot Meunier and 2023 Marine Layer Pinot Noir River Camp Ridge in a cool room or give a 20-minute fridge chill right before dinner so they hit 55–59°F.
  3. 1 Hour Before Dinner: Open and decant 2020 Altamura Sangiovese (~1 hour air softens youthful Wooden Valley grip).
  4. 30 Minutes Before Dinner: Open and decant 2022 Matthiasson Cabernet Franc (~30 minutes). Pull white and sparkling bottles from the fridge 15 minutes before pouring so aromatics bloom.
Kitchen Cooking Wine (2 Cups Total: Gravy & Cranberry Sauce)

The Turkey Gravy and Red Wine Cranberry Sauce each call for 1 cup of dry red wine. Best move: pick up one clean $12–$18 Côtes du Rhône, basic Pinot Noir, or Barbera for the stove—or, if pulling from your cellar, use your single bottle of 2023 Zumaya Ribera del Duero (~13.5% ABV, light 3-month oak). Keep all Napa/Washington Cabernets, Petit Verdots, Prisoner, and Amarone strictly out of the saucepan so heavy oak tannins don't turn bitter when reduced.
