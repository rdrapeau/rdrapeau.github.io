Research & Publications

Peer-reviewed papers in human-in-the-loop ML, crowdsourcing, and multimodal accessibility.

Microtalk: Argumentation in Crowdsourcing
AAAI HCOMP '16
Ryan Drapeau, Lydia B. Chilton, Jonathan Bragg, Daniel S. Weld
A peer argumentation protocol (Assess → Justify → Reconsider) where crowd workers exchange structured rationales, boosting individual and aggregate classification accuracy by +20% on challenging NLP tasks without ground truth.
Tactile Graphics with a Voice
ACM TACCESS '16
Catherine M. Baker, Lauren R. Milne, Ryan Drapeau, Jeffrey Scofield, Cynthia L. Bennett, Richard E. Ladner
A multimodal accessibility system combining physical relief graphics with smartphone camera QR scanning and finger-pointing guidance to provide localized audio explanations for complex STEM diagrams.
The Wisdom of Multiple Guesses
ACM EC '15
Johan Ugander, Ryan Drapeau, Carlos Guestrin
A crowdsourced forecasting mechanism that elicits multiple ranked guesses per individual to capture heterogeneous confidence distributions, creating certainty-weighted crowd aggregations that outperform standard mean estimates.

A mobile acoustic speech therapy system providing children with real-time phonological feedback and articulation game mechanics, while streaming phoneme accuracy telemetry to speech-language pathologists.
